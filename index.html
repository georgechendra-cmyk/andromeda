FAQ

Questions

Why not just use one model?

No single model is best at everything. Labs leapfrog each other every few weeks, and the best coder is rarely the best researcher or the best writer. Andromeda keeps track so you don't have to.

How does it decide?

A fast model reads every request and decides what would serve you (an answer, an app, a video, research...) and how to do it. Then each part goes to the models that score highest on exactly that work on LMArena's leaderboards. You can also choose Fast, Max, or one model yourself.

Can it use my computer?

Only if you run its small connector on your computer, and it asks before it changes anything. Without it, Andromeda still runs code, searches and builds right in your browser.

What does it cost?

Everyone with an account gets free credits that come back every day. When you want more, buy a pack once: no subscription.

Is my data safe?

Your chats, projects and memory stay in this browser. What you ask goes through Andromeda's server to the model answering it, and isn't kept there.

Which model answered me?

The best one for each part, picked for you. "Show models used" on any answer shows who did what, and why.
