FAQ

Questions

Why not just use one model?

No single model is best at everything. Labs leapfrog each other every few weeks, and the best coder is rarely the best researcher. Andromeda keeps track so you don't have to.

How does it decide where a message goes?

Instantly and without an extra model call: it reads what you wrote and what you attached (code, maths, a question about today, an image) and picks the current flagship of the lab that's strongest at that kind of task, within your price setting.

What does it cost?

Andromeda is free. You pay OpenRouter per token for paid models, and every answer shows its cost. Choose Free to use only $0 models.

Is my data safe?

There's no Andromeda server. Your key is stored in your browser and sent only to openrouter.ai with each request. Your chats stay in this browser.

Can I choose the model myself?

Yes. In Settings you can pin any model for any kind of task, or switch the whole thing to a cheaper tier.
